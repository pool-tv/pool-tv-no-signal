# Pool TV: No Signal

A movement-only arena survival roguelite. You are Mr. Pool — the weapons fire themselves, you just drive. Survive the channel, beat the boss at 15:00, unlock the next one.

21 fighters · 15 channels · a 60-piece collection of weapons and evolutions · hidden TVs · Endless & Late Night modes.

![Main menu](menu-screenshot.png)

## Install (Android)
1. Download the APK from the latest Release.
2. Open it and allow installing from your browser when Android asks.
3. Play — touchscreen or controller.

Free. Made by Pool Tv 🕳📺 Game Engine.
